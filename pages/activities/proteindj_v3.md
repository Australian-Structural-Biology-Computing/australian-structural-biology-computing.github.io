## Details

- [ProteinDJ](https://github.com/PapenfussLab/proteindj) is a modular Nextflow workflow that brings together the major stages of protein design: fold design, sequence design, structure prediction, and analysis.
- Although ProteinDJ is open source, some software components used by earlier releases had licence conditions that restricted commercial use and distribution.
- A collaboration between WEHI, Audax, and Australian BioCommons replaced the restricted components and contributed performance and usability improvements for the v3.0 release.
- The collaboration followed an [Australian Structural Biology Computing webinar](https://youtu.be/xwvF62HxaF0) about ProteinDJ in 2025.

## Completed

- Replaced the previously restricted software components with alternatives that allow commercial use.
- Merged and validated the replacement components alongside performance and usability improvements.
- Released ProteinDJ v3.0 through the [official ProteinDJ repository](https://github.com/PapenfussLab/proteindj).

The release also supports future incorporation of ProteinDJ into the [Australian BioCommons Structural Biology Platform](biocommons_structural_biology_platform).
