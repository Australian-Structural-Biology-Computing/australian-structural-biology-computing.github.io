## Details
 
A collaboration developed the content for a `Foundations of Protein Structure` module made available
through EMBL-EBI Training. The development of the module was a collaboration between members of the
Australian Structural Biology Computing community and the team from
[Protein Data Bank in Europe (PDBe)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pdbe/).
