## Details
 
Community members presented a [workshop](https://blackochrelabs.au/abacbs2025_workshops/) at the [2025 ABACBS conference](https://www.abacbs.org/abacbs2025) in Adelaide describing structural biology workflows on national HPC infrastructure.
 
### Abstract
 
Deep learning for structural biology has numerous applications, ranging from basic research through to clinical application. A key challenge with these approaches is accessibility. Building, executing, and sharing workflows becomes more difficult when implementing deep-learning methods and moving them to national infrastructure to operate at scale. The workshop examined these challenges across the workflow life cycle, combining theory and hands-on activities to help bioinformaticians develop, execute, and share computational structural biology workflows at scale.
